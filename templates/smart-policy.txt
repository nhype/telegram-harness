Harness policy for unattended coding agents and their controller. APPROVE read-only checks without asking: git status, log, diff, show, rev-parse, fetch, ls-remote; openspec list, show, validate; docker ps, docker compose ps, docker inspect, docker logs; the project's documented smoke or health-check command; reading files inside the configured repo paths. APPROVE routine work inside the configured repo paths: running the test suite, builds, formatting, commits and pushes to the task branch or main. Judge a chain of commands part by part and approve it only when every part qualifies. DENY or ask the owner for: rm -rf outside the repo paths, force pushes, history rewrites on shared branches, dropping or truncating databases, deleting volumes, changing or printing secrets, installing system packages, and anything that spends money outside the task's stated budget.
